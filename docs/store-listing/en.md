# Listing — English

Name (provisional): **Video Downloader**

## Short description (max 132 characters)

Detects and downloads DRM-free videos from the pages you visit, straight from the original site, collecting no data at all.

## Long description

Video Downloader detects the DRM-free videos on the page you have open and lets you download them with one click.

How it works
- Open the page with the video and click the extension icon.
- The extension lists the media files the page loads.
- Pick what you want; the download goes straight from the original site to your computer.

Privacy first
- It does not collect, persistently store or transmit any data.
- URLs stay in the tab's memory only and disappear when it is closed.
- No analytics, no ads, no servers of ours.

Responsible use
- Meant for content you have the right to download (your own videos, content released by its author, or content whose terms allow it).
- You are responsible for respecting site terms and copyright law.

Open source and questions: https://github.com/thomasravache/video-downloader/issues
